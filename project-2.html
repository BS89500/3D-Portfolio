the problem

Gig posters are usually designed by hand, one at a time. Paperwave lets a band drop in a track and get dozens of print-ready layouts shaped by that track.

Describe who the project was for, what was broken or missing, and what constraints you had to work within.

what i built

The audio is analysed in the browser with an FFT, and the spectrum drives colour, scale and rhythm of the shapes on the page. Exports are vector files at poster size.
