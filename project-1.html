the problem

Paddlers were checking three different sites to learn when a channel was safe to cross. Tidepool puts tide height, current speed and wind on one map, hour by hour.

Describe who the project was for, what was broken or missing, and what constraints you had to work within.

what i built

The map is drawn in WebGL so thousands of current vectors animate smoothly on a phone. Tide data is cached on the device so the app still works at the launch ramp with no signal.
